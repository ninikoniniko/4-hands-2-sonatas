overview:

"Four Hands, Two Sonatas" is a 2026 coming-of-age South Korean music drama that follows two contrasting piano prodigies and an exceptional violist navigating rivalry, friendship, and artistic growth over a decade.The Story: The series spans 10 years, starting at an elite arts high school and moving into the competitive world of adult classical music. Two rival pianists—one privileged but anxious, the other poor but rawly talented—transform their intense friction into a deep bond, learning to play together like a four-hands piano piece.
